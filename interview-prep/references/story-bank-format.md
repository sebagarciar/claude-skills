# Story bank format

`job_search/story-bank.md` is the narrative counterpart to `master-cv.md`. The CV bank holds
one-line claims with metrics. The story bank holds what happened around them: the tension, the
decision, the feedback, the thing that went wrong.

Two files, deliberately. `master-cv.md` stays a clean, tagged, one-line bullet bank that
`tailor-cv` selects from. Narrative in that file would end up in a CV.

## Entry format

```markdown
### <Short handle> — <Company>, <Year>
`tags` `here`

- **Situation.** One or two lines. Scope and what was at stake.
- **Tension.** Why it was hard. The disagreement, the constraint, the risk.
- **Action.** What Seba did, first person singular. Decisions, not activities.
- **Result.** The outcome with the number, or an explicit note that it is unquantified.
- **Lesson.** One line. What he would do differently, or what it changed in how he works.
- **Also answers.** Other question types this story can serve.
```

## Tag vocabulary

Shared with `master-cv.md` where it overlaps, plus the competencies a CV never shows:

`leadership` `people` `hiring` `underperformance` `feedback` `conflict` `influence`
`stakeholder` `cross-functional` `failure` `recovery` `ambiguity` `prioritization`
`ownership` `pressure` `deadline` `negotiation` `saying-no` `changed-my-mind`
`ethics` `culture` `mentoring` `communication` `bad-news`

## Rules

1. **His words, tightened.** Grammar and clarity only. Do not restyle into corporate prose. If
   it does not sound like him out loud, it is wrong.
2. **Numbers must match `master-cv.md`.** Where a story sits under a CV bullet, quote the same
   figure. Two different numbers for one project is a live risk in an interview.
3. **Unquantified is fine, invented is not.** Write "no metric captured" and move on.
4. **Failures earn their place.** A bank of wins cannot answer half the questions that get
   asked. Keep pushing for these.
5. **One entry per situation.** Cross-reference with `[[handle]]` rather than duplicating.
6. **Date-stamp anything that could go stale**, like an ongoing project or a pending outcome.

## Seeding

Seba adds entries himself as situations occur to him. The skill also writes entries during
Phase 2, straight from his answers. Both paths use this format, and every skill-written entry
gets shown to him before it is saved.
